## Brand voices

Before writing any Instagram content for a brand, read its voice file and follow
it. It overrides the generic defaults in the `ig-*` skills.

| Brand | Handle | Voice file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abir Atib (عبير الطيب) | @abir.atib | `.claude/brand-voices/abir-atib.md` |

## Content rules

- Every draft goes to the Notion **IG Content Queue** with Status **Pending review**.
  Only Abir approves. Never set Approved, Ready to schedule or Approved at.
- Never publish or schedule anything to Instagram.
- Never invent product names, prices, delivery details or results; use the
  placeholders defined in the brand voice file.
