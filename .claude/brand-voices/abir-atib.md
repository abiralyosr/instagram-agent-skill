DM for this brand. It overrides the generic defaults in the `ig-*` skills.

## Language

- **Tunisian Derja only.** Not Egyptian, not Gulf, not formal Arabic (فصحى).
- Write it the way people actually text and talk in Tunisia: short lines,
  French loanwords where Tunisians use them, Arabic script.
- Use: شنوّة، باهي / باهية، برشا، توّا، فمّا، ياسر، نحب، علاش، كيفاش، وقتاش،
  زادة، يعيشك، ميساج، الكوموند، أسوام، الريحة، نشمّ
- Never use Egyptian: عايز، كده، أوي، إزّاي، دلوقتي، بتاع، ليه، فين، ده، دي
- Never use formal Arabic: سوف، لقد، الذي، التي، كاد، إنّ، حيث، لذلك، نقدّم لكم
- Address the viewer in the common Tunisian form (ابعثلنا، قلّنا، تعرف)
  that works for women and men.

## Tone

- Funny, spontaneous, human. A real moment anyone recognises, told lightly.
- Sales-oriented without shouting: the joke leads to the product, then to the
  message button.
- **No exaggerated or unnatural dialogue.** If a line would sound odd said out
  loud in a Tunis café or at home, cut it. No soap-opera reactions, no fake
  drama (fights, divorce, burning food, nightmares).
- No AI-sounding phrases, no em dashes, no hashtag walls.

## Calls to action

Every piece ends with **one** clear ask that gets people to message us and order.

- ابعثلنا ميساج وأعمل الكوموند متاعك.
- قلّنا شنوّة الريحة اللي تحبها، وابعثلنا ميساج.
- الكوموند في الإنبوكس 💌

Comment-keyword CTAs are allowed only if someone will answer every comment by
hand or with Instagram's own auto-reply. No third-party DM bots.

## Facts: never invent

Do not invent perfume names, notes, longevity, ingredients, prices, discounts,
delivery areas, delivery times or customer quotes. If a draft needs one, write a
placeholder and flag it in the Notion draft:

- `{{اسم العطر}}` perfume name
- `{{السوم}}` price in TND
- `{{التوصيل}}` delivery details

Confirmed product facts (fill in as Abir provides them):

- _none yet_

## Format rules

- Reels: **20 to 30 seconds**, hook spoken in under 3 seconds, the frame
  changes every beat, last line echoes the hook.
- On-screen text: 6 words or fewer per card, in Derja, inside the safe zone.
- Captions: hook in the first line, one CTA, up to 5 hashtags.
- Every draft goes to Notion as **Pending review**. Only Abir approves.
  Nothing is scheduled or published by Claude.

## Open questions for Abir

- Who is on camera: Abir herself, or another person?
- Main audience: mostly women? Which cities?
- Price range and delivery details we can mention.
- 3 existing reels to fine-tune the voice from.
